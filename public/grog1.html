What exactly is grog?

'grog' is a glitch in my system. I'm being serious. It took me days to figure this out and I so wish that was some kind of a sick joke. I have the system set up to "update Earth" as if it's a day-one release of your favorite video game. When it works, it works well enough. 'grog' is an example of when it doesn't work as intended. Back in September during a routine update session, I misinputed one of the flags for the update command. My computer still runs on UE-DOS (Ultimate Eric DOS) so I have to do things the old-fashioned way. There's two flags for the kind of situation I had run headfirst into. '/mp' is for pushing it down the main pipeline.That's the Earth you, me, and everyone else knows. '/bp' is for the backup pipeline. It's a massive safety measure given the amount of lives at stake. I'm not trying to harm the general public in any form. The problem is that pushing an update coded for the main pipeline down the other will result in problems I can't fix. Glitches aren't even the worst of it but I do what I can to try and prevent it. Every update is run through millions of simulations to determine the best and worst outcomes, total [potential] deaths, possible world-ending catastrophies, etc. When the system works, it works really well.

I mean, I still remember watching it happen in real time. Everything was going fine. Exactly as intended until it hit the lock check. The lock check is the system's region lock minus the fact that I'm outside of all regions of the universe and multiverses. If it passes the lock check, it just goes through. But if it doesn't and if it's a small enough issue, the system can stop it before any damage is done. This update though was huge. Kinda found it funny to punish every single bigot by removing them from existance and sticking them into an inescapable tungsten cube. But as the lock check failed the alarms started blaring and we were all rushing to figure out what the fuck was happening... and then 'grog' exploded out of one of the council members and shot through the ceiling. None of us knew what they were or what this meant. The first thing I did was immediately restore the last backup. Nothing on Earth was affected thankfully but it did not get rid of them. I could only hope they weren't nearly as dangerous as... meef. But that's a story for another time.
